# HL7 ADT Messages

HL7 ADT messages can provide emergency department arrival and encounter information. Common segments include MSH for message metadata, PID for patient identifiers and demographics, PV1 for visit information, OBX for observations such as chief complaint or test result, and DG1 for diagnoses.

For surveillance use, analysts should validate message control identifiers, facility fields, visit locations, observation names, diagnosis mappings, and missing segment rates.

Synthetic messages in this research platform are illustrative and do not represent real patient data.
