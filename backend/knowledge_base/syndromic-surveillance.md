# Syndromic Surveillance

Syndromic surveillance monitors pre-diagnostic indicators such as emergency department visits, chief complaints, diagnosis text, and syndrome categories. It is useful for early awareness because ED visit patterns can move before confirmatory diagnoses or severity indicators are available.

Analysts should compare current syndrome counts with historical baselines, stratify by geography and age group, and review whether the timing is consistent with seasonality, holidays, or known reporting patterns.

Key interpretation steps include checking ED visits, test positivity, hospitalizations, deaths, facility contribution, message completeness, and chief complaint term changes.
