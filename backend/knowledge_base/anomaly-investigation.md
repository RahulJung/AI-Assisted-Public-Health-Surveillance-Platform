# Anomaly Investigation

An anomaly investigation should begin by confirming that the signal is reproducible across data cuts. Review the date of onset, region, facility contribution, affected age groups, syndrome definition, and baseline comparison.

Triangulate early indicators with severity indicators. A true public health signal often has a coherent pattern across ED visits, positivity, hospitalizations, geography, and age distribution, although severity indicators can lag.

Document methods, assumptions, and limitations in an investigation brief.
