# ED Visit Trends

Emergency department visit trends are early surveillance indicators. Analysts compare current visits with day-of-week adjusted baselines, recent moving averages, syndrome definitions, and facility-specific historical volume.

Larger facilities usually contribute more stable trends because random noise is diluted across higher encounter volume. Smaller facilities can show noisy changes that require careful comparison with regional peers.

ED visit increases are more concerning when they follow earlier test positivity changes and are seen across multiple facilities or age groups.
