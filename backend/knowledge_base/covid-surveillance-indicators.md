# COVID Surveillance Indicators

COVID-like activity should be reviewed with multiple indicators. Early indicators include ED visits for respiratory or COVID-like syndrome and test positivity. Severity indicators include hospitalizations and deaths, which may lag early indicators.

An increase in positivity before hospitalizations can be plausible. A rise in ED visits without changes in positivity may reflect non-infectious causes, coding changes, public concern, or data artifacts.

Stratification by age group and region helps identify whether the signal is broad community transmission or concentrated activity.
